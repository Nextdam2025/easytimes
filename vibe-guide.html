🌿 Indica

Relaxing, body-heavy effects. Great for winding down, chilling on the couch, or unwinding after a busy day of sightseeing.

☀️ Sativa

Uplifting, energetic, and social. A good match for daytime chats, music, or exploring the city afterwards.

⚖️ Hybrid

A balance of both worlds — relaxed but clear-headed. Ask staff which hybrid fits the mood you're after.

🧪 THC vs CBD

THC is the compound behind the "high." CBD is non-intoxicating and can take the edge off if things feel too strong.

🍪 Edibles Take Longer

Edibles can take 30–90 minutes to kick in. Give it time before having more — the effects arrive later but last longer.

💧 Stay Comfortable

Drink water, have a snack, and take breaks. Our cold drinks and homemade snacks are just one tap away.
